A webhook is an automated way for one system to tell another 
system that something happened. 

Our agent reads checkout failures and makes decisions:
├─ HIGH severity? Send ALERT
└─ LOW severity? Just LOG it

The agent writes that decision to Google Sheets.
But Google Sheets is just a database, nobody's watching it.

webhook is how we tell the ops team 'Hey, a decision was made!

Without webhook: Decision sits in a sheet, nobody knows
With webhook: Decision is sent to the ops team instantly → they act fast


A good prompt is one where nothing is assumed.

Every question the agent might ask is already answered:

What do I do? → Mission
What data do I have?→ Inputs
Have I done this before? → State
Is this a problem?"→ Logic
What do I write? → Outputs
Who receives it? → Integration
Does this really work? → Examples
