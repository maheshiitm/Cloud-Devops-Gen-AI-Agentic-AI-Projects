This is an optional resource and we strongly encourage learners to attempt this exercise before Class 1, as it will help you come a little more prepared. It’s completely okay if you’re unable to work on it—we will walk through and discuss the exercise after Week 1.



Purpose: Introduce a real-world use case to help you apply key Agentic AI concepts before starting the course.



Focus areas:

Decision support
Process automation
Personalized recommendations


Scenario: Explore how AI agents can analyze data, make decisions, and recommend actions to improve business processes and customer retention for a bank’s premium credit card customers.



What you’ll do:

Identify signals of at-risk customers
Design a basic AI agent workflow
Consider how to communicate and implement solutions


Approach:

A thought exercise — no coding required
Focus on how generative AI systems work in practice and where human decision-making fits


Goal: Spark curiosity and practical thinking about how AI can be used in your role.
