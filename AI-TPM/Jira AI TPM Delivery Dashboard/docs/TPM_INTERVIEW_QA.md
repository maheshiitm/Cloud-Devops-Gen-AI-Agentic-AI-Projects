# TPM Interview Questions & Model Answers

## 1. How do you manage a blocked story?
I make the dependency explicit, identify the upstream owner, quantify the impact, define mitigation, establish a checkpoint and communicate the delivery impact.

## 2. How do you prioritize work?
I consider customer impact, business value, urgency, risk reduction, dependencies and engineering effort, then validate trade-offs with Product and Engineering.

## 3. How do you handle an API dependency?
Define the interface contract, owners, authentication, schema, error handling and availability expectations. Use mocks where feasible and track readiness as a dated dependency.

## 4. How do you run a sprint?
Start with a clear goal, prioritized backlog, capacity and acceptance criteria. During execution monitor flow, blockers, risks and scope. Finish with review and retrospective.

## 5. How do you report status to executives?
Lead with outcome, progress, top risks, decisions required and next milestone. Use Jira evidence to support the narrative.

## 6. Risk vs issue?
A risk is a potential future event. An issue is already affecting delivery.

## 7. How do you manage scope creep?
Make the change visible, assess impact, obtain an explicit decision, and trade capacity or existing scope rather than allowing silent expansion.

## 8. What makes a good acceptance criterion?
It is observable, testable, unambiguous and focused on expected behavior/outcome.

## 9. How do you manage AI product delivery?
Track data readiness, model/API dependencies, evaluation metrics, safety, latency, monitoring and fallback behavior in addition to normal product delivery.

## 10. How do you manage cross-team dependencies?
Track upstream/downstream teams, owner, artifact, date, impact, checkpoint and escalation path. Link dependencies in Jira.

## 11. How do you measure sprint health?
Use sprint-goal progress, remaining work, flow, blockers, risk age, scope change and quality signals.

## 12. What would you automate next?
Risk aging alerts, dependency reminders, executive status summaries and engineering integration signals such as PR/deployment/test status.

## Project-specific interview story
The project demonstrates a real TPM pattern: an AI priority prediction story was linked to an API readiness risk. The risk contained impact, owner, mitigation and checkpoint information. A second dependency chain was modeled for AI routing rules. This shows proactive delivery management rather than waiting for a blocker to become a production problem.
